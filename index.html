🌊 How to Be a Reef Guardian

Your mission isn't chasing a high score—it is keeping living species alive and nurturing ecosystem equilibrium!

🎯 The Core Objective

Maintain balanced water chemistry and coral coverage so baseline pioneer species thrive. As your Biodiversity Index rises, more delicate, keystone, and endangered species migrate into your sanctuary!

🎮 Essential Strategies:

  • 🔥 Combating Marine Heatwaves: If water temp surpasses 30.2°C, corals begin bleaching. Immediately deploy Solar Shade Screens or start Cold Upwelling Pumps.
  • 🗑️ Cleaning Plastic Pollution: Click drifting bottles and nets directly on canvas to earn immediate funds, or dispatch a Volunteer Cleanup Dive.
  • ⭐ Stopping Crown-of-Thorns Starfish (COTS): Click COTS directly to target-cull them, or reintroduce Giant Triton Snails for natural biological defense!
  • 🌿 Controlling Smothering Algae: Keep Rainbow Parrotfish and Blue Tangs healthy—their active grazing cleans bare rock so baby corals can settle!
  • 🛡️ Overfishing & Poachers: Maintain high MPA protection with Ranger Patrols to shield sharks and turtles from trawlers.
  • 🐬 Beneficial Allies: Giant Clams filter hundreds of liters of water daily, while Cleaner Wrasses run health clinics that boost disease immunity for all fish!
💡 Pro Tip: Click directly on swimming fish or turtles in the ocean at any time to open their live health card and read fascinating fun facts!
