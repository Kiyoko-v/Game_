👁️ How Cats See The World
🎨

Dichromatic Vision (No Red / Green)

Cats possess only two types of color-detecting cones: sensitive to Blue (~450nm) and Yellow (~555nm). Red appears as charcoal/dark slate, and green looks pale yellowish-gray!

🌙

Tapetum Lucidum & Night Vision

A reflective membrane behind the cat retina reflects light back through photoreceptors, allowing cats to see in light 6 to 8 times dimmer than humans can.

⚡

Supercharged Motion Tracking

Feline eyes have a high concentration of rod cells with rapid refresh rates, making them extraordinary at spotting fast-moving prey (and dodging flying obstacles!).
